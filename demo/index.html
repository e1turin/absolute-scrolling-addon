A different way to move

The whole page.
Within reach.

Hold your middle mouse button and move in any direction. A small movement takes you proportionally through the entire page.

20% cursor travel → 20% scroll range

Stop your hand. The page stops too.
Release to finish. Press again to start a new drag.

Load the extension before trying this page. The default vertical multiplier is 1×; horizontal is 4× for quicker movement across wide content.

01

Position, directly.

Each gesture starts wherever you are. Move down to travel further into the document; move up to go back.

02

Still means still.

Hold the button without moving. The document stays put, even if your cursor is far from where the drag began.

03

Your point of reference.

Return the cursor to where you pressed the button, and you return to your original scroll position.

04

Every direction.

Try the vertical panel on the right, or middle-drag sideways in the strip below. Horizontal movement maps to the viewport width. The gesture stays with the nearest scrollable area.
